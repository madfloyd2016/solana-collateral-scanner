# v0.5 regression coverage map

Date: 2026-11-06

This document maps the four-protocol audit summary supplied for this project to
v0.5 regression behavior. It does not claim that the production repositories
were re-fetched, re-scanned, dynamically reproduced, or matched to deployed
binaries during the v0.5 build.

## Implemented v0.5 rules

1. Parse `#[derive(Accounts)]` structs and field-level `#[account(...)]` attributes.
2. Record `has_one`, `constraint`, `token::mint`, `associated_token::mint`, `address`, `owner`, `seeds`, and `bump` evidence.
3. Treat a token-account Mint constraint plus a config `has_one` or Mint-address binding to the same Mint account as a structured identity chain.
4. Detect Token-2022 extension names and distinguish a local rejection/allowlist signal from an unresolved policy.
5. Report financial naked subtraction and checked/saturating/wrapping arithmetic semantics for review.
6. Include financial `*_checks` helpers as dataflow roots.
7. Track numeric/untyped asset selectors through simple indexed config selection and into recognized sinks.
8. Emit a dedicated `flash_repay` account-flow coverage marker.
9. Emit a low-severity hygiene lead for Anchor Mint account fields with no recognized local attribute binding.

## Explicit limitations

- The fixtures under `sample/v05_regressions/` are synthetic protocol-inspired examples, not copied production source.
- Anchor attribute parsing is structural, not a proc-macro expansion or compiler proof.
- `has_one`, `token::mint`, `address`, and PDA seed patterns require manual confirmation of actual account roles and runtime execution order.
- The selector pass is heuristic and currently focuses on direct indexing and common `get`/`nth` forms; arbitrary match dispatch, traits, macro-generated selectors, cross-crate aliases, and dynamic dispatch may be missed.
- Arithmetic patterns are review leads; surrounding range checks and state invariants may make them safe.
- Extension names do not prove that a live Mint has that extension or that the authority can exercise it.
- `flash_repay` is a coverage marker, not a vulnerability finding.
- Source-to-deployment equivalence is not verified by this report.
